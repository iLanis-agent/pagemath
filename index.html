Boundary: arithmetic only Page Math does whole-number pagination arithmetic on the numbers you type. It does not know how any real database, API or website pages its results, and it gives no verdict on whether your paging is right or fast. Every figure is your own entry.

The page truth

103 items at 10 per page fill 11 pages. The last page holds 3 items. Page 11 shows items 101 to 103 (OFFSET 100, LIMIT 10).

The locate truth

Item 101 at 10 per page is on page 11, number 1 on that page. Its zero-based index is 100 and that page starts at OFFSET 100.

The window truth

On page 11 of 20, a window of 5 links covers pages 9 to 13. A separate link to page 1 is needed. A separate link to page 20 is needed.
